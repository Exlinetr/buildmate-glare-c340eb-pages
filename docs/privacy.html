How Glare collects, uses, and protects your information.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how exlinetr (we, us, or our) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Glare mobile application (the App). We respect your privacy and aim to be clear about what we do with your data.

By using Glare, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App. This policy is provided as general information and does not constitute legal advice.

Definitions

The following terms are used throughout this Privacy Policy:

  • App: the Glare mobile application for iOS and Android, including any updates.
  • Personal Data: any information that identifies or can reasonably identify an individual.
  • Usage Data: information collected automatically when you use the App, such as feature interactions, crash logs, and device type.
  • Service Providers: third parties that process data on our behalf to help operate, analyze, or improve the App.
  • You: the individual using the App.

In short

We collect only the information Glare needs to work well and to improve it. We do not sell your Personal Data.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Glare and which features you choose to enable.

Information You Provide

You may give us information directly, for example when you contact support or use features that require input.

  • Your email address and message content when you contact us.
  • Account details, if a feature requires an account.
  • Content you create or upload within the App, if applicable.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use Glare, some technical information may be collected automatically.

  • Device model, operating system version, and language settings.
  • App version, crash reports, and performance diagnostics.
  • General usage events, such as which screens are opened.

Device Permissions

Some features may ask for access to your device, such as photos, camera, or notifications. Access is used only for the feature you requested, and you can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

Your photos and files

If you grant Glare access to photos or files, they are used only to provide the feature you selected. We do not use your photos for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Purchase Information

If you make an in-app purchase, payment is processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We may receive confirmation of the transaction to unlock purchased features.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain Glare.
  • To unlock and restore purchased features, if applicable.
  • To respond to support requests and feedback.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • To understand how features are used so we can improve the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and users.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

Glare may use trusted third-party Service Providers for analytics, crash reporting, and purchase handling. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only as needed to perform services for us.

Analytics data is used in aggregated or pseudonymous form where possible. We do not sell your Personal Data or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

  • Platform services from Apple and Google, including app distribution and in-app purchases.
  • Analytics and crash reporting tools that help us improve stability and features.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and for reasonable record-keeping. Diagnostic and analytics data is retained for a limited period and then deleted or anonymized. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include encrypted connections where data is transmitted and limited access to data.

Security note

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We work hard to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your device and accounts protected with strong passcodes.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your Personal Data. To exercise them, contact us at support@exlinetr.com.

  • Access the Personal Data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your Personal Data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Manage device permissions and notifications in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

Glare is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children. If you believe a child has given us Personal Data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the Last Updated date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App. Continuing to use Glare after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
