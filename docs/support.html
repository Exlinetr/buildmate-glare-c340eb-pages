Frequently Asked Questions

What is Glare?
Glare is a mobile app by exlinetr designed to be simple, fast, and easy to use. Open the app and follow the on-screen guidance to get started. Most features are available right from the main screen.
How do I get started with Glare?
Download Glare from the App Store or Google Play, open it, and follow the short onboarding steps. If the app asks for permissions, such as notifications or access to photos, you can choose whether to allow them. You can change these choices later in your device settings.
Do I need an account to use Glare?
Many parts of Glare can be used without creating an account. If a feature requires sign-in, the app will tell you before you continue. You can contact us at any time to ask about the data linked to your account or to request its deletion.
Does Glare cost anything?
Glare is free to download. If any optional paid features or in-app purchases are offered, their current prices and terms are shown in the app and on the store before you buy. All payments are processed by Apple or Google, not by us.
How do I restore a previous purchase?
If Glare offers in-app purchases, open the app settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the original purchase. If the purchase still does not appear, contact us and include your purchase receipt.
Is my data safe with Glare?
We take privacy seriously. Glare only collects what it needs to work and to improve the app. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds for purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com for iOS or through your Google Play order history for Android.
How can I send feedback or suggest a feature?
We would love to hear from you. Email us at support@exlinetr.com with your idea, feedback, or bug report. Screenshots and your device model help us understand your request faster.

Troubleshooting

The app crashes or freezes
Close Glare completely and reopen it. If the problem continues, restart your device and make sure you have the latest version of Glare from the App Store or Google Play.
Content is not loading
Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection and try again. Switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, or turning Airplane Mode on and off, often resolves temporary network issues.
A feature asks for permission again
Go to your device Settings, find Glare, and review its permissions. Allow the permission the feature needs, then return to the app and try again.
My purchase is not showing
Make sure you are signed in with the same store account used for the purchase, then tap Restore Purchases in the app settings. If it still does not appear, email us with your receipt.
Notifications are not arriving
Check that notifications are enabled for Glare in your device Settings and that Focus or Do Not Disturb modes are not silencing them.
The app is using too much storage
Update Glare to the latest version. If storage use stays high, you can delete and reinstall the app. Restore any purchases afterward if needed.
